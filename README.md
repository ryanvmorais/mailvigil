# MailVigil

**MailVigil reads the DMARC aggregate reports (RUA) your mail providers send you and tells you,
in plain language, who is sending mail as your domain and whether it is safe to move your policy
to `p=reject`.**

## Status

Under development. Nothing is released yet, and the interface will change until the first
version is out.
